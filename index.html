Requires organized business records — what that means

Which one am I?

You're Complete if you itemize, sold investments, own a rental property, or received a K-1, 1095-A, or HSA form. You're Self-Employed if you had freelance or business income. Otherwise, you're Essential. Unusually complex situations (several rentals, multiple businesses) may be quoted higher — we'll always confirm your price before we start.
