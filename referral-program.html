Terms & Conditions

Eligibility: Open to existing clients and professional partners of Hennig Financial LLC.

New clients only: The person referred must not have used Hennig Financial services in the past 24 months.

Payout timing: Rewards are paid within 30 days after the referred client pays their first invoice in full.

Business referrals: The $50 reward applies to new accounting retainer or payroll clients.

Retainer discount: The 15% retainer discount applies to the first three (3) months of service only.

Self-referrals: You cannot refer yourself or a business you primarily own.

Timing: Your name must be provided on the intake form. Retroactive referrals are not guaranteed.

Changes: Hennig Financial LLC may modify or end this program at any time.
