About Hennig Financial

Modern financial services built on transparency, technology, and trust.

Our Mission

We started Hennig Financial LLC to solve a simple problem: small business owners and individuals often feel underserved by traditional firms. They face hourly billing surprises, outdated systems, and a lack of proactive communication.

Our approach is different. We combine **flat-rate, transparent pricing** with modern technology to provide stress-free tax preparation, bookkeeping, and payroll services. We believe you should know exactly what you're paying for and feel confident that your financials are in expert hands.
