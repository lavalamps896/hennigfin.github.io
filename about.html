About Hennig Financial

A small New Jersey practice serving individuals, small businesses, and nonprofits.

Why we exist

Small business owners and families are often underserved by traditional firms: hourly billing surprises, slow responses, and an accountant they only hear from in April.

We publish our prices, handle everything through a secure online portal, and stay in touch year-round. For businesses, that means help with bookkeeping, payroll, taxes, and managing employees, so you understand your numbers all year instead of just at filing time.
