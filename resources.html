Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about who we are, what we charge, and how it works.

Credentials & Scope

Are you a CPA firm or a law firm?+

No. Hennig Financial LLC is neither a CPA firm nor a law firm. We provide tax preparation, bookkeeping, payroll, and business support services. We do not provide legal advice or financial statement audit or review services.

What is the AFSP Record of Completion?+

The IRS Annual Filing Season Program (AFSP) is a voluntary program for tax preparers who complete annual continuing education, including a federal tax law refresher course and exam, and agree to follow IRS practice standards. Ron holds a Record of Completion for the 2027 filing season and renews it every year.

Can you represent me if the IRS contacts me?+

For returns we prepared and signed, yes — within limits. As an AFSP Record of Completion holder, Ron can represent you before IRS revenue agents (examinations), customer service representatives, and the Taxpayer Advocate Service.

We cannot represent you before IRS Appeals or Collections, or on returns we didn't prepare. For those situations, we'll help you organize documents and refer you to a CPA, enrolled agent, or attorney.

Who is not a good fit for your services?+

We focus on individuals and small businesses. We can't take on clients who need audited or reviewed financial statements, have annual revenue over $1 million, are publicly traded, or have complex international tax situations.

Tax Preparation

Which tax package do I need?+

Essential ($150): W-2s, unemployment, interest and dividends, Social Security, simple retirement income, the standard deduction, dependents, EITC, and education credits.

Complete ($225): choose this if you itemize, sold investments or crypto, own a rental property, or received a K-1, 1095-A, or HSA form.

Self-Employed ($350): choose this if you had freelance or business income and your records are organized.

Every package includes your federal return and all state returns. Not sure? We'll confirm your package and price on your onboarding call before any work begins.

What counts as “organized records” for the Self-Employed package?+

A profit & loss summary or bookkeeping report showing your income and your expenses totaled by category. If you'd be handing us raw bank statements and receipts to sort through, you'll need a cleanup project first — or an accounting retainer so it's handled all year.

Can I pay my fee out of my refund?+

Yes. Through our bank partner, EPS Financial, your preparation fee can be deducted from your federal refund so you pay nothing up front. EPS charges a $39.95 processing fee for this service, which is also deducted from your refund. We do not offer refund advances or refund loans. We'll walk you through the details before you sign.

Do you prepare business and nonprofit returns?+

Yes. S-corporation (1120-S) and partnership (1065) returns start at $750 and are included with any accounting retainer. We also prepare Form 990-EZ and 990 for nonprofits, with reduced community pricing for small, volunteer-run organizations.

Accounting & Payroll

Do I need QuickBooks?+

No. If you already use QuickBooks Online, we'll work in your file. If you'd rather not pay for software, we'll maintain your books in our own system and send you the reports.

How do retainers work?+

Retainers are a flat monthly fee for a 12-month engagement. Your business tax return is included. If service ends before 12 months, the return is billed at our standard rate. “From” prices depend on your transaction volume and number of accounts, and we'll confirm your exact monthly rate before you start.

Why do messy records cost more?+

Our flat prices assume records are current. If we need to categorize months of transactions or reconcile accounts from scratch, that's a separate cleanup project, quoted up front after a free review.

Do you hold my payroll tax money?+

No. Payroll taxes are paid directly from your business bank account. We handle the calculations, filings, and deadlines, but we never hold your funds.

Getting Started

How do I get started?+

Fill out our short intake form. Ron or Mikaela will follow up to schedule a 15-minute call, confirm your price, and send you access to our secure client portal.

How do I send you my documents?+

Through our secure client portal — never by regular email. Please don't include Social Security numbers or tax documents in the intake form; we'll collect those securely once you're set up.
